# Endfall art: style guide and generator

All sprites are generated by code: `node tools/art/generate.mjs` writes `public/art/atlas.png` and
`public/art/atlas.json` (PixiJS spritesheet format with animations). The output is committed so the game runs
without regenerating; rerun the generator after changing anything in `tools/art/`. To replace the art with hand-made
sprites, keep the frame names below and the sizes/anchors, and write your own atlas in the same format.

## Look

- Top-down pixel art at native resolution: 32 px tiles, units 20–36 px, buildings fill their footprint.
- 1 px dark outline (`#0b0f16`) around every sprite except glows and effects.
- One light, from the top-left, applied in world space after rotation: every facing of a unit is lit the same way.
- 3–6 colour ramps per material (dark → light, base tone in the middle). No anti-aliasing inside sprites; smooth
  gradients only for additive lights (`light`) and the fog layer.
- Buildings are seen from above with a short south wall (5–9 px) for depth.
- Team colour carries identity: amber (player) and magenta (opponent) on hulls and trim. Everything else is
  steel, concrete and dark glass so team colour, shards (teal) and ether (violet) read first.

## How the generator draws (tools/art/raster.js)

Shapes (polygons, ellipses, rings, thick lines) are filled by testing pixel centres, so edges are crisp at any
rotation. Every pixel stores a material and a height. `shade()` then picks a ramp step per pixel: higher than the
up-left neighbour → lighter, higher than the down-right neighbour → darker, tucked beside something taller →
darker (ambient occlusion), plus a per-material surface pattern (hazard stripes, concrete slabs, panel seams).
Empty pixels next to filled ones become the outline.

## Frames

| Name | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ground_0..5` | 32 | dual-grid cell with no rock; 1–5 add pebbles/cracks away from the edges |
| `rock_<mask>_<v>` | 32 | dual-grid cell; mask bits 1 TL, 2 TR, 4 BL, 8 BR = corner is rock; 3 variants that all join |
| `shard_0..3` | 32 | crystal cluster, full → nearly mined out |
| `vent_0..3` | 64 | ether vent swirl animation |
| `<unit>_<team>_<dir>_<frame>` | 32 / 48 | harvester (2 frames), trooper (4), vanguard (4); dir 0–15, 0 = facing +x, clockwise |
| `bldg_<type>_<team>` | footprint | core 4×4, depot/extractor/turret 2×2, barracks/forge 3×3 tiles |
| `turret_head_<team>_<dir>` | 40 | rotating gun, 16 facings |
| `foundation_<n>`, `scaffold_<n>`, `rubble_<n>` | n×32 | construction and destruction, n = 2, 3, 4 |
| `boom_big_0..7`, `boom_small_0..5` | 64 / 28 | explosions |
| `puff_0..3`, `flash_0..2`, `spark_0..3` | 16 / 16 / 8 | smoke, muzzle flash, hit spark |
| `flag_0..3` | 16×20 | rally flag |
| `sel_<r>_<0|1>` | — | selection ellipses, green (own) / red (other), r = 12, 18, 26 |
| `bolt`, `shell`, `light` | — | projectiles and the additive light blob |

Animations in atlas.json: `boom_big`, `boom_small`, `puff`, `flash`, `spark`, `flag`, `vent`, and
`<unit>_<team>_<dir>` walk cycles.
