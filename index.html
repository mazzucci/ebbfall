ENDFALL

A browser RTS in the 1998 mold. One faction, one map, one opponent. Pre-alpha.

Mine shards and ether, build supply, raise an army, and level the enemy Command Core before their waves level yours. The enemy base is in the far corner.

  • Mouse: left-click or drag to select, right-click to move, attack, gather or set a rally point. A attack-move, S stop, H hold. Ctrl+1–9 saves a control group. Double-click selects all of that unit type on screen. . idle Harvester, Q whole army, Space jump to the last attack, P pause.
  • Touch: tap to select; tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack, tap a shard field to gather. Drag the map with one finger to look around (flick to glide), pinch to zoom. Hold SELECT (or tap it) and drag to select a group; double-tap a unit for all of its type. Drag on the minimap to nudge the view, tap it to jump. With a building selected, a tap on the ground lets go of it; its rally point is set with Rally. Tap a shard field or vent (with nothing selected) to see what is left.
  • Select a Harvester and tap Build to place structures. The Ether Extractor must sit on an ether vent; then send Harvesters to the Extractor to mine ether. Harvesters also repair damaged buildings (the Repair button, or right-click the building); repairs cost a quarter of the price for a full fix.

On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and open Endfall from there to play full screen in landscape.
