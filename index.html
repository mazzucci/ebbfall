ENDFALL

A browser RTS in the 1998 mold. One faction, one map, one opponent. Pre-alpha.

Mine shards and ether, build supply, raise an army, and level the enemy Command Core before their waves level yours. The enemy base is in the far corner.

  • Mouse: left-click or drag to select, right-click to move, attack, gather or set a rally point. A attack-move, S stop, H hold. Ctrl+1–9 saves a control group.
  • Touch: tap or drag a box to select; tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack, tap a shard field to gather. Two fingers pan. Drag on the minimap to scroll.
  • Select a Harvester and tap Build to place structures. The Ether Extractor must sit on an ether vent.
