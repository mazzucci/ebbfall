HOW TO PLAY

  • Select with a click or a drag; right-click to move, attack, mine or set a rally point.
  • Each button shows its key: A attack-move, S stop, H hold in Classic; in WASD, a key by its place on the card.
  • Q your whole army, Space jump to the attack, Ctrl+1–9 save a group, P pause.
  • The arrow keys, the screen's edge or the middle button move the map; Ctrl+wheel zooms.
  • Tap to select; tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a mine to mine.
  • Hold SELECT and drag for a group; double-tap a unit for all of its type.
  • ARMY selects your whole army, HOME brings you home.
  • Drag with one finger to look around, pinch to zoom; hold a button to see what it does.
