ABOUT

Ebbfall is a real-time strategy game in the spirit of the 1998 classics, made for fun and played in the browser, on a phone or a desktop. Two races, up to eight players, and friends over the Internet.

Made by Stelian Matei. Everything is generated by code: the 3D-rendered sprites, the painted terrain, the synthesized sounds and voices. The game is a deterministic simulation in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.

How to play

Mine shards and ether, build supply, raise an army, and level the enemy Command Core before their waves level yours. The enemy base is on the far side of the map.

  • Mouse: left-click or drag to select, right-click to move, attack, gather or set a rally point; hold the middle button and drag to move the map. In the panel, click a unit for it alone, Shift+click to drop it. Double-click selects all of that unit type on screen (on a factory: all of them, and they train as one).
  • Keyboard, in one of two schemes (change them in OPTIONS, on the menu's first page or the pause menu). Classic: the arrow keys or the screen's edge move the map (WASD too while nothing is selected), and each button's letter is its key: A attack-move, S stop, H hold. WASD: WASD always move the map, and a button's key is its place on the card: ERT the top row, FGH the middle one, CVB the bottom one (each button shows its key). In both: Shift with a unit's key trains five, Ctrl+1–9 saves a control group, . idle Harvester, , idle factory, Q whole army (twice: look at it), Space jump to the last attack (again: the ones before), Home back to base, Esc cancel or let go, P pause.
  • Touch: tap to select; tap the ground to move, tap an enemy to attack, tap a shard field to gather. Drag the map with one finger to look around (flick to glide), pinch to zoom. Hold SELECT (or tap it) and drag to select a group; double-tap a unit for all of its type, or a factory for all of them (they train as one, the shortest queue first). Drag on the minimap to nudge the view, tap it to jump; the house button beside SELECT brings you home, and a second tap on the army chip looks at your army. With a building selected, a tap on the ground lets go of it; its rally point is set with Rally. Harvesters given a job (gather, build, repair) are let go, so your next tap does not pull them off it; after placing a building the Build list reopens for the next one. Tap a shard field or vent (with nothing selected) to see what is left; a worker left idle for a while pings the minimap. With a group selected, tap a type in the panel at the bottom to keep only those, hold it to drop them. Tap a queued unit (in the panel) to cancel it; hold any command button to see what it does and costs.
  • Select a Harvester and tap Build to place structures (the Foundry, Airfield and a new Core are under Advanced). The Ether Rig must sit on an ether vent; then send Harvesters to the Rig to mine ether. One Harvester mines a field or vent at a time: two or three per field is plenty, and a Harvester that finds its field busy moves to a quieter one nearby. A vent runs dry in time (after that it only trickles), so take the vents at other bases too. Harvesters also repair damaged buildings (the Repair button, or right-click the building); repairs cost a quarter of the price for a full fix.
