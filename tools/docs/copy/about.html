About Ebbfall

Ebbfall is a free real-time strategy game for the browser, on a phone or a desktop.

How it's built

  • The game is a simulation in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly: it runs in your browser, with no game server.
  • Multiplayer is deterministic lockstep: every device runs the same game, tick for tick, and only your orders travel, through a small relay that passes them along. That is how a phone and a laptop play in the same match.
{{diagram network}}
  • The art is 3D models rendered once into sprites, drawn with WebGL (PixiJS). The terrain, the sounds and the voices are generated by code. No game engine, no framework.
  • Once loaded, the game plays offline, and installs as an app.
  • Free and open source under the MIT license: github.com/mazzucci/ebbfall.

Privacy

  • No account, no ads, no cookies, no trackers.
  • Your settings stay in your browser, on your device.
  • Cloudflare counts visits to the game's page without cookies: how many, not who.
  • In multiplayer, your orders pass through a small relay to the other players: see How it's built.
