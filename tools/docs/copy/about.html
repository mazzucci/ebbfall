About Ebbfall

Ebbfall is a free real-time strategy game for the browser, on a phone or a desktop.

How it is made

Everything is generated by code: the 3D-rendered sprites, the painted terrain, the synthesized sounds and voices.

The game is a deterministic simulation in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly. Multiplayer runs the same simulation in lockstep on every player's device, through a small relay.

Privacy

Ebbfall is a game, not a service. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing of yours is sold.

  • No account, no login, no ads, no cookies, no trackers. The page loads nothing from anyone but Cloudflare, which hosts the site and the relay.
  • Your settings, your name for online play, your record against the AI and the game's files for offline play stay in your browser, on your device. Clear the site's data and they are gone.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the game's page: how many, from which countries and kinds of device, and which site sent you. It sets no cookie and follows nobody.
  • In multiplayer, a small relay passes your orders to the other players. It sees your connection's address, to slow spam and keep out removed players, and forgets it within about half an hour after the room goes quiet (the spam counters within a couple of hours).
