About Ebbfall

Ebbfall is a free real-time strategy game for the browser, on a phone or a desktop, made for fun. In 2047, time travel was first attempted. An expedition jumped six centuries back, into a medieval kingdom that wasn't expecting company.

The Expedition brought bots, buggies, drones and a big dish, hired the locals, and dug in. The Kingdom has castles, archers, knights and a very long memory.

Who and how

Made by Stelian Matei. Everything is generated by code: the 3D-rendered sprites, the painted terrain, the synthesized sounds and voices.

The game is a deterministic simulation in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly. Multiplayer runs the same simulation in lockstep on every player's device, through a small relay.

Playing

  • Against one to seven computer players at three levels: Hard gathers a little faster than you; Easy and Medium get no bonus.
  • With friends online: open a room and send its link. No account, nothing to install.
  • Offline, once the page has been opened; INSTALL on the menu makes it an app. PLAY OFFLINE on the menu says whether it is all there.
