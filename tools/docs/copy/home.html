Ways to play

  • Against one to seven computer players, each at its own level: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • Free for all or in teams of any shape, on eight maps of two sizes.
  • With friends over the Internet: open a room, send its link, computer players fill the empty seats.
  • Offline, once the page has opened; INSTALL on the menu makes it an app.
