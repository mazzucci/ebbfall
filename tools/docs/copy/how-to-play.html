The field manual

How to play

Mine gold, build, grow your population, raise an army, and bring down the enemy's base before their waves bring down yours. Everything below is the game as it plays today.

The goal

{{diagram loop}}

Workers mine gold. Gold raises buildings and trains an army. The army brings down the enemy's Castle or Data center.

A side is out when its last building falls, or when it has nothing left to fight with and no gold to train more. The enemy's base is across the map.

TUTORIAL on the menu walks you through a first game.

More touch habits
  • Workers given a job (mine, build, repair, or a building placed) are let go, so your next tap does not pull them off it.
  • A finger that moves before the ring fills pans instead; a second finger cancels the hold.
  • While an order waits for your tap, its button reads Cancel: tap it to think again.
  • Tap a gold mine with nothing selected to see how much gold is left.
  • A worker left idle for a while pings the minimap.
  • With a group selected, tap a type in the panel to keep only those; hold it to drop them.
  • Tap a queued unit in the panel to cancel it.
  • No saved groups on a phone: ARMY, a box and the panel's types are your groups.

Gold and workers

{{diagram economy}}

Every base has a gold mine a few steps from its headquarters. Workers mine only when you send them: select them, then tap the mine (or right-click it).

  • A new worker waits by your Castle or Data center, or walks to the mine its rally point is set on.
  • Up to {{w villagersPerMine}} Villagers, or {{w botsPerMine}} Bots, work a mine at once; more wait their turn, and a worker at a crowded mine moves to a quieter one nearby.
  • A worker who finishes a building waits beside it. Send it back.

Gold by itself. The Kingdom's Market earns about a gold every {{w marketSeconds}} seconds with no workers, one per Castle; Trade research raises it. The Expedition's Wind turbines generate energy by themselves, up to {{w turbinesPerHq}} per Data center.

A new base. Build a Castle or a Data center by another gold mine. The headquarters keeps a few tiles off the mine; the game shows you where it fits.

Building

Select a worker and tap Build. What needs another building first is under Advanced, which opens once your Barracks (the Expedition: Recruiting post) stands; each building shows up as it opens.

  • Villagers repair damaged buildings (the Repair button, or right-click the building): a quarter of the price for a full fix.
  • A Bot stays at each building until it stands, and repairs buildings too (not machines).
  • A building that lost its builder waits, half built, until a worker returns to it.

Population and the grid

{{diagram cap}}

Every unit needs a place. The Kingdom calls it population: the Castle gives {{n castlePop}}, each Cottage {{n cottagePop}}, up to {{n popMax}}. The Expedition calls it the grid: the Data center gives {{n dataCenterGrid}}, each Substation {{n substationGrid}}, up to {{n gridMax}}.

When the number at the top turns red and pulses, not even a worker fits: build a Cottage or a Substation before you train more.

The Expedition is different

{{diagram expedition}}

Energy builds things; the grid keeps them running. Bots dig thorium from the same mines, and the Data center's small reactor turns it into energy. Energy is spent where the Kingdom spends gold; the Recruiting post prints coins from it to pay the locals.

Machines need signal. Buggies, Drones, Rovers and Androids work only inside the coverage of a Data center ({{n dataCenterCoverage}} tiles) or an Uplink ({{n uplinkCoverage}} tiles), and move faster there. Sellswords and Bruisers are hired locals: they fight anywhere.

Building inside the signal. The Expedition's buildings go up only inside coverage. A new Data center and an Uplink can go up anywhere you have explored, and a building keeps working if its coverage is lost later.

No armor. Its units are deadly but fragile. The Lab's Energy efficiency makes its machines cheaper instead.

The path to the Portal. The Lab unlocks the Uplink, and the Uplink the Portal: one per player, and it teleports only while an Uplink stands.

How to win

{{figure shot-battle}}
  • Scout and hold. The enemy's first wave comes early. A Watchtower or a Sentry mast by the mine, and a few soldiers, hold it.
  • Spend the gold. A bank wins nothing. More Barracks or Recruiting posts train faster.
  • Expand. A second mine doubles your income; defend it.
  • Then go. Select the army (ARMY or Q), attack-move into the enemy base, and bring down the Castle or the Data center.

The levels. Easy: small, slow attacks that give up quickly, for learning. Medium: the whole game at an even pace, with no bonus. Hard: attacks sooner and harder, and gathers a little faster than you.

Pause. P or PAUSE shows the clock. Against a friend the game goes on under it; PAUSE FOR EVERYONE stops it for both (three a game, five minutes at most). After a win against the computer, SHARE sends a friend the same game as a challenge.
