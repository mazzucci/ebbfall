About Ebbfall

Ebbfall is a free real-time strategy game in the spirit of the 1998 classics, made for fun and played in the browser, on a phone or a desktop.

Two races, the human colonists and the alien Vael. Play against one to {{maxComputers}} computer opponents at {{levelCount}} levels, or with friends online: open a room and send its link, no account, nothing to install. Once opened, single player works offline too, and the game installs as an app.

Made by Stelian Matei. Everything is generated by code: the 3D-rendered sprites, the painted terrain, the synthesized sounds and voices. The game is a deterministic simulation in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.
