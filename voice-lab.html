EBBFALL VOICE LAB

Options for the announcer ("Base under attack", "Research complete", "Not enough shards/ether"). It is off in the game until one of them sounds right. Turn the sound on (on an iPhone, the ring/silent switch to ring) and tap a phrase.

A, B and C are the game's own synthesizer: no recordings, a buzz and a hiss shaped like a mouth. D is this device's own speech voice, which differs from phone to phone: pick another of its voices to try it.
