EBBFALL VOICE LAB

Options for the announcer ("Base under attack", "Research complete", "Not enough gold"). It is off in the game until one of them sounds right. Turn the sound on (on an iPhone, the ring/silent switch to ring) and tap a phrase.

A, B and C are the game's own synthesizer: no recordings, a buzz and a hiss shaped like a mouth. D is this device's own speech voice, which differs from phone to phone: pick another of its voices to try it.
